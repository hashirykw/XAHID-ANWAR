A marked answer, start to finish

A narrative composition (1123, Paper 2, Section B). Tap a highlighted line to read the comment.

CompositionWrite a story that begins: "The lights went out just as..."

The lights went out just as Ammi lifted the biryani pot. For a moment nobody moved. The ceiling fan sighed to a stop, and the heat crept back into the room like a cat that had only pretended to leave.

Outside, the generators of the whole street started one by one, they were very loud and very noisy. Dadi began to recite something under her breath. My little brother found the torch and shone it straight into my eyes, delighted.

"Somebody check the meter," Abbu said, in the voice he used for things that were definitely somebody else's job.

That was when we heard the knock. Three slow knocks, then nothing, then three more. Nobody we knew knocked like that.

Tap a highlightEach highlighted line has a comment from the checker.

Composition

Band 4 of 5 · Practice

Approved by Sir
Content and structure13/15
Style and accuracy8/10
  • The opening drops the reader straight into the moment and the family feels real.
  • Similes and small details (the torch, Abbu's voice) show character without explaining it.
  • "Very loud and very noisy" repeats one idea. Pick one precise word: roared, stuttered.
  • A comma joins two full sentences in paragraph two. Use a full stop or a semicolon.

Outside, the street's generators coughed awake one after another, until the whole lane droned like an engine room. Dadi's lips moved silently. My brother found the torch and, delighted, aimed it straight at my face.

Lovely ending hook. Next time, build the suspense for one more paragraph before the knock. 21 stands. — Sir X
