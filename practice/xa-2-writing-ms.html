Mark scheme

Section A: Directed Writing [25]

15 marks for content and evaluation, 10 marks for style and accuracy.

ContentDescription
13 to 15Evaluates ideas from both texts with insight (e.g. distraction vs. safety and learning use); a clear, convincing personal view; practical suggestions for the school; letter format and tone suit a head teacher throughout.
9 to 12Uses ideas from both texts with some evaluation; clear view; some suggestions; mostly appropriate format.
5 to 8Repeats ideas from the texts with little evaluation; view stated but undeveloped; one text may dominate.
1 to 4Limited use of the texts; little sense of purpose or audience.

Ideas students may evaluate: distraction in class; lost conversation at breaks; phones as learning tools; parents' need to contact children; whether a ban removes the problem or just hides it; middle-ground options (phones handed in, used only in lessons with permission, a phone policy taught to students).

Style and accuracyDescription
9 to 10Precise, varied vocabulary; persuasive, well-linked paragraphs; very few errors.
6 to 8Clear, organised writing with some variety; occasional errors that do not affect meaning.
3 to 5Simple, generally clear; frequent errors in places.
0 to 2Errors often obscure meaning.

Section B: Composition [25]

15 marks for content and structure, 10 marks for style and accuracy.

Content and structureDescription
13 to 15Descriptive: a strong sense of place built from precise sensory detail; controlled focus and pace; little or no plot. Narrative: engaging, well-shaped story with tension and a satisfying ending; for Q4 the given ending lands naturally.
9 to 12Relevant and organised; some well-chosen detail or a clear plot; occasional loss of focus.
5 to 8Relevant but uneven; description becomes a list, or the story is mostly events with little development.
1 to 4Limited relevance or organisation.
Style and accuracyDescription
9 to 10Wide, precise vocabulary; varied sentences used for effect; very few errors.
6 to 8Some ambitious vocabulary and sentence variety; mostly accurate.
3 to 5Plain vocabulary; repetitive sentences; noticeable errors.
0 to 2Errors often obscure meaning.
