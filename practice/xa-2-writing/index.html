XA Practice Paper 2 · Writing

O Level English Language 1123 format · 2 hours · 50 marks

Sir Xahid Anwar
Instructions
  • Answer Section A and one question from Section B.
  • Section A: write about 250 to 350 words. Section B: write about 350 to 450 words.
  • Plan your answers. Leave time to check spelling and punctuation.

Section A: Directed Writing

Your school is deciding whether to ban mobile phones completely during the school day. The head teacher has asked students for their views.

Text 1: From a parents' group newsletter

Phones in class mean constant distraction. Teachers say students check messages under the desk, and break times are spent staring at screens instead of talking. A full ban would give children six hours a day free from notifications.

Text 2: From a student blog

My phone is my dictionary, my calculator and the way my mother checks I've reached school safely. Banning phones won't stop distraction; teaching us to use them properly would. Some of my best project research happened on my phone in the library.

  1. Write a letter to your head teacher giving your view on whether phones should be banned during the school day. In your letter you should:
    • evaluate the ideas in both texts
    • give your own view, with reasons
    • suggest what the school should do.
    Base your letter on what you have read, but use your own words. Address both texts. [25]

Section B: Composition

Answer one question.

Descriptive writing

  1. Describe a railway station in the moments before a long-distance train departs. [25]
  2. Describe a place that looks completely different at night from how it looks in the day. [25]

Narrative writing

  1. Write a story that ends with the words: "...and that was the last time anyone opened that door." [25]
  2. Write a story about a mistake that turned out to be lucky. [25]

End of paper. Total: 50 marks.
