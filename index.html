    Meet Sir Xahid

    Sir Xahid Anwar teaches Cambridge English Language for O Level 1123 and IGCSE 0500, 0510 and 0511. Mornings, he teaches in school classrooms. Evenings, he takes batches at academies from North Nazimabad to Bahadurabad. Online, his students join from wherever they are.

    His whole method sits in three words on his logo.

    1. Read

      Pull meaning out of a passage: what it says, what it hints at, and why the writer chose those exact words.

    2. Write

      Plan and shape directed writing and compositions the way Cambridge examiners reward them.

    3. Repeat

      Practise against the clock, get marked, fix one thing, then go again until it sticks.
